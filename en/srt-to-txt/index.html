SRT to TXT: Subtitles to Plain Text

Strip numbers and timestamps in one click and keep only the dialogue — perfect for transcripts, meeting notes and study material. VTT, ASS and SBV work too.

🔒 Files are processed in your browser and never uploaded



How to use

  1. Add subtitle files: drag SRT, VTT or ASS files into the box.
  2. Adjust options: join line breaks and remove duplicates; the preview updates live.
  3. Download or copy: save as TXT or click "Copy all text" to paste into a document, notes app or AI tool.

What gets removed

Besides dialogue, an SRT file contains cue numbers, timestamps, blank lines and formatting tags such as italics. Converting to TXT removes all of that:

SRTTXT
1
00:00:01,000 --> 00:00:03,500
<i>Hello everyone,</i>
welcome back.		Hello everyone, welcome back.

Popular uses

  • Transcripts — turn YouTube, course or meeting captions into readable, searchable text.
  • Summarising with AI — plain text is much shorter than a subtitle file, so it fits more easily into AI tools.
  • Language study — collect a show's dialogue for reading, flashcards and review.

Why remove duplicates?

Auto-generated captions often repeat the same words across neighbouring cues to create a rolling effect. Removing consecutive duplicates keeps each line once, giving a much cleaner transcript.

FAQ

Does SRT to TXT keep timestamps?

No. Only the dialogue is kept; numbers, timestamps and formatting tags are removed.

Can I convert VTT or ASS?

Yes. SRT, VTT, ASS, SSA and SBV are all supported, and ASS override tags are stripped automatically.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser and your files never leave your device.
