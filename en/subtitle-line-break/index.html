Subtitle Line Breaker

Subtitles too long for a phone screen? Set a maximum line length. Lines are wrapped at punctuation and natural pauses, and anything still too long is split into separate cues with the time shared out by length.

🔒 Files are processed in your browser and never uploaded

How to use

  1. Pick a preset: English subtitles use about 42 letters and up to two lines. Chinese vertical dramas usually use 12 characters on one line. You can also set your own numbers.
  2. Choose your subtitle files: SRT exported from CapCut, AI dubbing or translation tools all work. Garbled encodings are fixed automatically.
  3. Compare and download: the dark preview on the right shows roughly how it looks on a phone. Download and import it back into your editor.

Why vertical video needs shorter lines

A portrait phone screen is about half as wide as landscape video, so far fewer characters fit on a line. When a subtitle is too long, editors wrap it onto three or four lines and cover the speaker's face, or the text has to shrink until viewers can't read it in time. Short, evenly paced lines keep people watching.

FAQ

How many characters per line for vertical video?

Phone screens in portrait are narrow. For Chinese, 10–14 characters on a single line is a good target. For English, keep lines to about 32–42 characters. Landscape video can use up to two longer lines.

How is the timing worked out when a long line is split?

The original duration is shared out in proportion to the length of each part. A 6-second line split into three equal parts gets 2 seconds each, back to back, with no gaps or overlaps.

Will English words be cut in half?

No. English words are kept whole and only broken at spaces. Latin letters and digits count as half the width of a Chinese character.

Are my subtitle files uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser and your files never leave your device.

Does it keep ASS styling?

No. The output is always SRT, so ASS styles and effect tags are removed. If you need styled subtitles, break the lines first, then apply styles in your editor.
