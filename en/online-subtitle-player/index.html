How to use

  1. Choose files: drop the video and subtitle into the box together, or one after the other.
  2. Play: subtitles appear on the video; garbled encodings are fixed automatically.
  3. Adjust: subtitles early or late? Use −0.1s / +0.1s or the G / H keys. Change text size or convert Chinese as needed.
  4. Save: click "Download fixed SRT" to keep the corrected timing for your TV or other players.

When is this useful?

  • Check whether a downloaded subtitle is in sync before copying it to your TV.
  • Watch a video with subtitles on a computer without VLC or other players installed.
  • Proofread translations, or test subtitles exported from CapCut or YouTube.
  • Play VTT files online on a work or school computer where you can't install software.

FAQ

Are my video and subtitle uploaded?

No. Both files are read and played inside your browser and never leave your computer or phone, so even large videos start instantly.

Why won't my video play?

The player uses your browser's built-in decoders. MP4 (H.264) and WebM work almost everywhere; HEVC/H.265 and some MKV files may not. Try Chrome or Edge, or convert the video to MP4.

Can I play VTT subtitles online?

Yes. SRT, VTT (WebVTT), ASS, SSA and SBV are supported. ASS styling and positioning are not kept; lines are shown as regular subtitles.

What if the subtitles are garbled?

Nothing to do: the player detects GBK, Big5, Shift-JIS, EUC-KR and other encodings automatically. The downloaded SRT is UTF-8.

Can I save the timing changes?

Yes. Click "Download fixed SRT" to save a subtitle file with your timing shift and Chinese conversion applied, ready for any player.
