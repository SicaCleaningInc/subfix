About ZimuFix

ZimuFix is a free toolbox built for Chinese subtitles. The most common problems — garbled text, Simplified versus Traditional characters, subtitles out of sync — usually mean jumping between several websites, and many tools make you upload your files.

So every ZimuFix tool runs inside your browser. Your subtitle files are never sent to a server, which is faster and keeps them private.

Open source

The CJK subtitle encoding detector used on this site is open source: cjk-subtitle-encoding on GitHub.
