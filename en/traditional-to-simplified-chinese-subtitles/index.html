Traditional to Simplified Chinese Subtitles

Convert SRT, ASS and VTT subtitles from Traditional to Simplified Chinese, with optional Taiwan-to-mainland vocabulary conversion. Old Big5 files are handled automatically.

🔒 Files are processed in your browser and never uploaded

How to use

  1. Choose an option: "Characters only" for Hong Kong subtitles; "Taiwan vocabulary → mainland" if the subtitles are for a mainland audience.
  2. Add your subtitles: drag files into the box. Big5-encoded files are read automatically.
  3. Compare and download: check the preview, then download individually or as a ZIP.

The two options compared

Using the sentence 這個軟體的影片很好看 ("this software's videos are great"):

OptionResult
Characters only这个软体的影片很好看
Taiwan vocabulary → mainland这个软件的视频很好看

The vocabulary option also handles terms like 資訊 → 信息 (information), 印表機 → 打印机 (printer), 滑鼠 → 鼠标 (mouse), 計程車 → 出租车 (taxi) and 記憶體 → 内存 (memory).

FAQ

What is the difference between the two options?

"Characters only" turns Traditional characters into Simplified ones, so 軟體 becomes 软体. "Taiwan vocabulary → mainland" also converts wording, so 軟體 becomes 软件 and 影片 becomes 视频.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Conversion happens entirely in your browser.

Will ASS fonts and effects break?

No. Only the dialogue text is converted; styles, font names and override tags stay the same.

Can it read old Big5 subtitles?

Yes. Big5, GBK and UTF-8 are detected automatically, and the output is UTF-8.
