ASS to SRT Converter: Convert ASS & SSA Subtitles Online

Convert ASS / SSA fansub subtitles to SRT. Override tags, drawings and comments are removed automatically so the file plays on TVs, phones and editors.

🔒 Files are processed in your browser and never uploaded

How to use

  1. Add ASS files: drag .ass or .ssa files into the box — a whole season is fine.
  2. Check the preview: the line count is shown next to each file name.
  3. Download SRT: the name is kept, only the extension changes, so players still match the video.

What the converter handles

  • Override tags in curly braces such as {\an8}, {\fad(200,200)} and {\fn...} are removed.
  • Line breaks: \N becomes a real line break.
  • Comments: lines starting with Comment: are skipped.
  • Drawings: vector shapes drawn with \p1 are skipped by default.
  • Sorting: ASS events are not always in time order, so they are re-sorted and numbered.

What you lose

SRT is plain text, so fonts, colours, positions, outlines, karaoke and animations disappear and the player's default style is used. If your computer player supports ASS (VLC, mpv, PotPlayer), keep the original for the full effect and convert only for TVs, phones or editing software.

How to convert ASS to SRT

  1. Drop your .ass or .ssa files into the box above. You can add a whole season at once.
  2. Check the preview to make sure the dialogue looks right.
  3. Download the SRT files, or all of them as one ZIP.

Why convert ASS to SRT?

  • TVs and set-top boxes often don't support ASS, or show the raw tags. SRT works on almost every TV.
  • Video editors such as Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve and CapCut import SRT, not ASS.
  • YouTube and most platforms accept SRT uploads.
  • Phones and media servers handle SRT more reliably, and Plex doesn't need to burn it in.

FAQ

Does ASS to SRT keep the effects?

No. SRT only supports plain text, so styling and animation are removed; dialogue and timing are kept.

Why are there fewer lines after converting?

Comments, drawing commands and empty events are skipped because they are not on-screen dialogue.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser and your files never leave your device.

Is converting ASS to SRT free?

Yes. The converter is free with no sign-up, no watermark and no file limit. Everything runs in your browser.

Will karaoke and sign lines be removed?

Vector drawings and Comment lines are skipped. Karaoke and sign lines that contain text are kept as plain subtitles, without their effects.

My ASS file has Chinese text that comes out garbled. What should I do?

The file is probably saved as GBK or Big5. Run the SRT through the Garbled Subtitle Fixer to convert it to UTF-8.
