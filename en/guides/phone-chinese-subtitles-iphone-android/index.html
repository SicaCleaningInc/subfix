How to Play External Chinese Subtitles on iPhone & Android

Updated: 2026-11-18

The built-in Photos or video apps on phones generally cannot load external SRT subtitles. The easiest way is the free VLC app with the video and subtitle side by side.

Step 1: Install VLC

Search for "VLC" in the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android) and install the official VLC for Mobile. It is free and has no ads.

B

Step 2: Copy the video and subtitle to your phone

  • iPhone: use the Files app to put both files in VLC's folder, use Finder file sharing from a Mac, or use VLC's Wi-Fi upload feature.
  • Android: copy both files into the same folder on the phone.

Give the subtitle the same name as the video, e.g. Movie.mp4 and Movie.srt, and VLC loads it automatically.

C

Step 3: Garbled subtitles

VLC for Mobile has a Subtitles → Text Encoding option in its settings. Try GB18030 for Simplified Chinese or Big5 for Traditional Chinese.

Even simpler: open the Garbled Subtitle Fixer in your phone's browser, pick the subtitle, and download the fixed version. Nothing is uploaded.

FAQ

Can the iPhone Photos app show SRT subtitles?

No. Use VLC or another player that supports external subtitles.

How do I get files into VLC on iPhone without a computer?

Save them to the Files app (for example from email or a cloud drive), then share or move them into VLC's folder.

Can I watch with subtitles in the phone browser?

Yes. Open the online subtitle player, pick the video and subtitle, and play. Nothing is uploaded.

Subtitles are too small on my phone. Can I change that?

VLC lets you change the subtitle size in its settings. The online player also has size buttons.
