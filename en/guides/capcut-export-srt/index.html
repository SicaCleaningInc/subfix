How to Export SRT Subtitles From CapCut Desktop

Updated: 2026-11-20

CapCut's auto captions save a lot of typing. Exporting them as an SRT lets you reuse them on YouTube, convert Chinese scripts, or build bilingual subtitles.

A

Before you start

  • Use the desktop app. SRT export is mainly a desktop feature; the mobile app generally has no option to export a subtitle file. The Chinese version (剪映) works much the same way.
  • Versions change. CapCut updates often, so button names and positions may differ, and some features may require a paid plan.
B

Steps in CapCut desktop

  1. Generate captions: drag the video to the timeline, open Text (or Captions), and choose auto captions. CapCut creates a caption track.
  2. Proofread: fix mistakes in CapCut first; the SRT keeps your edits.
  3. Open Export at the top right, or press Ctrl + E.
  4. Export captions only: untick video export if you only need the file, tick captions export, and choose SRT.
  5. Pick a folder and export. You'll get an .srt file.
C

What to do with the SRT

Convert Simplified to Traditional Chinese

Chinese captions come out in Simplified script. For Hong Kong or Taiwan viewers, use the Simplified to Traditional converter; the Taiwan option also swaps vocabulary such as 视频 → 影片.

Re-wrap lines for vertical video

Auto captions can be long and cover the actors on a vertical screen. The subtitle line-break tool (Chinese interface) splits long lines and re-times them by length.

Make bilingual subtitles

Translate the file, then combine both languages with the bilingual subtitle merger.

Fix timing

Reusing captions on a version with an intro? Shift them with the Subtitle Time Shift tool.

Get a plain transcript

Strip the timecodes with SRT to TXT for scripts, blog posts or AI summaries.

D

Importing the SRT back into CapCut

In CapCut's Text or Captions panel, choose to import local captions and select the processed SRT. You can then apply one style to all lines.

FAQ

Can I export SRT from the CapCut mobile app?

Generally not directly. Open the project in CapCut desktop to export captions.

Why is my exported SRT garbled in another program?

Some older programs expect a different encoding. Run the file through the Garbled Subtitle Fixer to save it as UTF-8 with BOM.

Can I upload the SRT to YouTube?

Yes. See uploading Chinese subtitles to YouTube.
