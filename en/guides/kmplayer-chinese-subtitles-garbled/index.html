How to Fix Garbled Chinese Subtitles in KMPlayer

Updated: 2026-11-20

KMPlayer is a long-standing player for Windows and phones. Older GBK or Big5 subtitles can still show up garbled. Here's how to fix it.

Fix 1: Convert the subtitle to UTF-8 (recommended)

Drop the file into the Garbled Subtitle Fixer. It detects the encoding and converts to UTF-8; replace the original and it works in KMPlayer and every other player.

B

Fix 2: Change the encoding in KMPlayer

  1. Press F2 to open Preferences.
  2. In the subtitle settings, find the language or encoding option (the location varies between versions).
  3. Try Chinese Simplified (GBK) for Simplified subtitles or Chinese Traditional (Big5) for Traditional ones.
  4. Reload the subtitle.
C

Boxes instead of characters

Boxes mean the font has no Chinese characters. Pick a Chinese font such as Microsoft JhengHei or Microsoft YaHei in the subtitle font settings.

D

Subtitle not showing

  • Same name as the video, same folder: naming rules.
  • Or drag the subtitle file onto the player window.

FAQ

Does KMPlayer for Android have the same problem?

Yes, the cause is the same. Converting the subtitle to UTF-8 fixes it on every version.

Can KMPlayer show two subtitles at once?

Some versions support a secondary subtitle, but merging both languages into one file with the bilingual merger works everywhere.

Subtitles are early or late in KMPlayer. What now?

Adjust the delay in the player for one viewing, or fix the file with the Subtitle Time Shift tool.
