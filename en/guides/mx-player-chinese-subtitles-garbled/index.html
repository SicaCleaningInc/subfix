How to Fix Garbled Chinese Subtitles in MX Player

Updated: 2026-11-18

Watching a video in MX Player on your Android phone or tablet and the subtitles turn into unreadable symbols? It is an encoding problem, and there are two ways to fix it.

Fix 1: Change the character encoding in MX Player

  1. During playback, open the Subtitle menu.
  2. Go to the subtitle settings and find Character encoding (the location varies between versions).
  3. Try GBK or GB18030 for Simplified Chinese, or Big5 for Traditional Chinese.

If it is still garbled, you probably picked the wrong encoding; try the other one.

B

Fix 2: Convert the subtitle to UTF-8 (permanent)

  1. Open the Garbled Subtitle Fixer in your phone's browser.
  2. Select the subtitle file. The preview shows the correctly decoded text.
  3. Download the converted file and replace the original.

Everything happens in the browser on your phone; the file is not uploaded anywhere.

C

Subtitle not loading automatically?

  • Keep the subtitle in the same folder as the video, with the same file name.
  • Browser downloads usually land in the Download folder; move the subtitle next to the video.
  • Or load it manually from the Subtitle → Open menu during playback.
D

Subtitles too early or too late

MX Player can adjust the delay, but only for the current session. Fix the file itself with the Subtitle Time Shift tool.

FAQ

Is there a free alternative to MX Player?

VLC for Android is free and ad-free, and also has a subtitle encoding setting.

MX Player doesn't find subtitles in my Download folder. Why?

MX Player looks next to the video. Move the subtitle into the video's folder with the same name, or open it manually from the subtitle menu.

Can I make subtitles bigger in MX Player?

Yes, in the subtitle settings you can change size, colour and background. These don't affect the file itself.

Will converting to UTF-8 work for Japanese or Korean subtitles?

Yes. Use the Japanese & Korean fixer.
