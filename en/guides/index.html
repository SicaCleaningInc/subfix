Step-by-step fixes for Chinese, Japanese and Korean subtitle problems on popular players and devices.

How to Fix Garbled Chinese Subtitles in PotPlayer

Chinese subtitles in PotPlayer showing gibberish, question marks or boxes? Check the subtitle encoding and font, or convert the file to UTF-8 once and for all.

How to Fix Chinese Subtitles in Kodi (Boxes or Garbled Text)

Chinese subtitles in Kodi showing as boxes or gibberish? Switch the font to Arial based, set the subtitle character set, and convert subtitles to UTF-8 for good.

How to Fix Garbled Chinese Subtitles in MX Player

Chinese subtitles in MX Player showing gibberish? Change the character encoding in the subtitle settings, or convert the file to UTF-8 so it works on any Android device.

Fix Chinese Subtitles on Xiaomi TV, Android TV & Google TV

Chinese subtitles garbled, showing boxes or not appearing on a Xiaomi, TCL, Sony or other Android TV / Google TV? Check encoding, file names and format, or switch to a better player.

How to Play External Chinese Subtitles on iPhone & Android

Want to watch a video with an external SRT subtitle on your iPhone or Android phone? Use VLC to load subtitles, set the text encoding, and fix garbled Chinese subtitles right on your phone.

Fix Garbled Chinese Subtitles When Uploading SRT to YouTube

Uploaded an SRT to YouTube and the Chinese text is garbled or out of sync? Convert the file to UTF-8, fix the timing, and upload subtitles correctly in YouTube Studio.

Fix Garbled Chinese SRT Subtitles in Premiere Pro & DaVinci Resolve

Imported an SRT into Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve and the Chinese text is garbled or shows boxes? Convert the file to UTF-8 and set a Chinese font.
