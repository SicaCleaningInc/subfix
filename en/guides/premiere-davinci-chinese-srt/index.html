Fix Garbled Chinese SRT Subtitles in Premiere Pro & DaVinci Resolve

Updated: 2026-11-18

Importing a ready-made SRT saves a lot of editing time. But in Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve, Chinese text sometimes comes in garbled or as boxes. There are two causes: encoding and font.

Quick fix: Auto-detects GBK, Big5, Shift-JIS and EUC-KR and converts to UTF-8. Files never leave your device.
Open Garbled Subtitle Fixer

Cause 1: The subtitle is not UTF-8

Editing software generally expects SRT files in UTF-8. GBK or Big5 subtitles come in garbled, usually with no option to change the encoding.

Fix: convert the file with the Garbled Subtitle Fixer and import it again.

Cause 2: The font has no Chinese characters

Boxes mean the caption track's font does not support Chinese.

  • Premiere Pro: select the captions and change the font in the Essential Graphics or Text panel to a Chinese font such as Noto Sans CJK, Source Han Sans, Microsoft JhengHei or PingFang.
  • DaVinci Resolve: select the subtitle track and change the font in the Inspector.

Panel names may differ between versions.

ASS subtitles won't import

Most editors do not support ASS directly. Convert it with the ASS to SRT converter first.

Lines too long for vertical video

For TikTok, Reels or Shorts, long lines run off the screen. The subtitle line-break tool (Chinese interface) wraps each line to a set width before you import.
