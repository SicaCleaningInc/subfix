Fix Garbled Japanese & Korean Subtitles

Anime or K-drama subtitles turned into symbols? Drop the file below. We detect Shift-JIS, EUC-KR and other encodings automatically and convert to UTF-8 so they play on any device.

🔒 Files are processed in your browser and never uploaded

How to use

  1. Choose your subtitle files: drag garbled Japanese or Korean subtitles into the box. A whole season at once is fine.
  2. Check the preview: the detected encoding is shown next to each file, with the first few lines of dialogue.
  3. Download: the file keeps its original name — replace the old one and your player picks it up.

Why Japanese and Korean subtitles break

Older Japanese subtitles are usually saved in Shift-JIS, and older Korean ones in EUC-KR (or its Windows extension CP949). Players that expect UTF-8 — most smart TVs, phones and many web players — read the bytes with the wrong rules and display nonsense. The text is not damaged; it just needs converting.

Fixing it in VLC instead

Go to Tools → Preferences, set "Show settings" to All, open Input / Codecs → Subtitle codecs → Subtitles and set "Subtitle text encoding" to Japanese (Shift-JIS) or Korean (EUC-KR/CP949). Save and reopen the video. The setting applies to every subtitle, so converting the file once is usually easier.

FAQ

Which encodings are detected automatically?

UTF-8, UTF-16, Shift-JIS (Japanese, including Windows CP932), EUC-KR / CP949 (Korean), GBK and Big5 (Chinese). If detection picks the wrong one, choose another from the dropdown and the preview updates instantly.

Are my subtitle files uploaded?

No. Everything runs inside your browser. Your files never leave your computer or phone.

Will the file name change?

No. The fixed file keeps its original name, so your player still matches it to the video.

What about EUC-JP or ISO-2022-JP files?

These older Japanese encodings are rare in subtitle files and are not detected automatically. Most Japanese subtitles use Shift-JIS or UTF-8.

It shows empty squares instead of Japanese or Korean.

The encoding is fine, but your player is using a font without those characters. Choose a font such as Meiryo or Yu Gothic for Japanese, or Malgun Gothic for Korean, in your player settings.
